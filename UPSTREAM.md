# Upstream provenance

Based on https://github.com/xushengfeng/lime (GPL-3.0).
Imported upstream commit: fb1ebdbe0744766a7a7395f218d1305d2e920664.
Upstream source is retained under `lime/`, and its Git history is preserved.
The root-level GPL-3.0 license also covers our modifications.

Local additions: Windows ARM64 Hexagon NPU transport through GenieX,
recent committed-context retokenization, Rime named-pipe client and flat theme,
profiling, automatic candidate/context tests, and workspace setup scripts.
Models and GenieX/Weasel/runtime binaries are downloaded separately and are
not redistributed by this repository; refer to their respective licenses.
